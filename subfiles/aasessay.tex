\textbf{1. Why did you choose your proposed course and institution?}

The Ph.D. program I am interested in pursuing is in architecture at the University of Queensland (UQ).
\textbf{I aim to enroll in this course because it offers programs that focus on design excellence, sustainability and community engagement,} making it well-positioned to provide valuable insight and expertise for equitable development in Indonesia.
This development has become a high priority for the Indonesian Government due to the continuous concentration of many developments on Java Island.

The symbolic of this development is the planned capital city of Nusantara (IKN).
It is located right at the center of Indonesia, so it can ensure fair and inclusive distribution of benefits among all regions.
Although the capital city has not been completely built, the developments have been taken place in some developing areas. For example, the Makassar- Parepare train has been operating routes that span 110 kilometer.

\textbf{I also enrolled in this course because it involves collaborating with leading researchers} whose work aligns with mine.
Most of their research revolve around city planning and social sustainability, which will prepare me to develop a dissertation centered on the identity of public spaces.
My dissertation hopefully can propose a policy to developing city in achieve equity with other big cities.
I will benefit not only from their research, but also from the relationship with them, which will enable us to generate innovative ideas together, even after graduation.

Furthermore, I chose the UQ because it has contributed to  Indonesia through alumni, collaboration and agreement, from which I can learn. The contribution will provide me with comprehensive overview of best practice to achieve goals.
Therefore I can implemented it through my role or project involvement to advance infrastructure need of Indonesia.
